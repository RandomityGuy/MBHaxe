# 2.0.0
PlatinumQuest port initial Release. This was not expected to ever be done and yet here we are!  
This port consists only of PlatinumQuest campaign and support for all the PQ custom levels that do not use custom code. Certain liberties were made during design to ensure the experience is smooth across different platforms while trying to remain faithful to the original game.
For those not knowing what PlatinumQuest is:  
PlatinumQuest is a massive community-built update and official mod to the Marble Blast series, featuring over 130 levels and power-ups like water, ice shards, and cannons alongside a ton of other new gameplay mechanics.  
Overall, for the best experience you should download and play the original one at MarbleBlast.com instead of this port if you have a computer that can play it.
